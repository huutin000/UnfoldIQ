# UNFOLDIQ Policy & Rights Gate (STEP 09 V2)

Core file: logic and invariants only.
It is NOT the permanent source of platform-specific truth — official current
sources (`policy/SOURCES.yaml`) and verified snapshots override it.

## Rights Invariants

- A public URL is NOT a license.
- Downloadable does NOT mean permitted.
- A few seconds is NOT automatic fair use.
- Copyright/rights review and reused-content review are SEPARATE gates.
- AI disclosure does NOT legalize impersonation or deception.
- Generated reconstruction is NOT evidence.
- UNKNOWN material rights CANNOT silently pass.
- Legal-exception claims → `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, never a guaranteed legal conclusion.

## Rights Source Types

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ORIGINAL` | Created by the producer for this video |
| `AI_GENERATED` | Generated by an AI provider for this video |
| `LICENSED` | Commercial/stock license held |
| `PERMISSION` | Direct permission from the rights holder |
| `CREATIVE_COMMONS` | CC license with terms recorded |
| `PUBLIC_DOMAIN` | Public domain with basis recorded |
| `USER_PROVIDED` | Supplied by the user (user warrants rights) |
| `COPYRIGHT_EXCEPTION_CLAIM` | Exception claimed but not adjudicated |
| `UNKNOWN` | No known basis |

## Rights Decisions

- `VERIFIED` — basis recorded and sufficient for the planned use.
- `REVIEW_REQUIRED` — human/legal review needed before publish.
- `BLOCKED` — must not ship in this form.
- `NOT_APPLICABLE` — no third-party/AI/likeness material in scope.

## AI Disclosure — YouTube Baseline (snapshot-tied)

Per current official YouTube disclosure guidance (source `YT-AI-DISCLOSURE`,
surface `YT-HOW-CONTENT-MADE`): realistic/photorealistic GenAI must be disclosed when it
makes a real person appear to say/do something they did not, alters footage of a
real event/place, or generates a realistic scene that did not occur.
Non-realistic, minor, or production-assistance uses are not automatically
disclosure-required. These are normalized rules tied to a policy snapshot —
not permanent hard-coded truth.

## AI Disclosure — TikTok Baseline (snapshot-tied)

TikTok maintains AI-generated/edited-media transparency rules and continues AI
labeling/transparency work (sources `TT-INTEGRITY-AUTHENTICITY`,
`TT-AI-TRANSPARENCY-2026`). Normalized rule: realistic AIGC requires
transparency/label review; prohibited or misleading AIGC does NOT become
acceptable merely through a label; unoriginal/AI spam can trigger
integrity/distribution concerns. Snapshot, not permanent truth.

## Reused / Inauthentic / Unoriginal Content (separate from copyright)

YouTube monetization policy treats repetitive/mass-produced/generic content and
reused content with insufficient original value as potentially ineligible —
including repetitive low-value content, templated storylines, slideshows with
minimal narrative/educational value, generic/mass-produced AI output, and
readings of material the creator did not create.
UNFOLDIQ Editorial Value signals feed this risk rating:

- `LOW_RISK` — substantive original educational value.
- `REVIEW_REQUIRED` — borderline originality.
- `HIGH_RISK` — templated/repetitive low-value output.

Never claim monetization is guaranteed. TikTok routing additionally considers
unoriginal content, AI-generated spam, and feed-eligibility/integrity rules.

## Historical / Scientific Reconstruction

| Classification | Rule |
|---|---|
| `EVIDENCE_ASSET` | Actual source/evidence with provenance and rights. |
| `RECONSTRUCTION` | Generated/staged plausible scene. NEVER masquerades as documentary evidence. |
| `ILLUSTRATION` | Explanatory visual (diagram, chart, abstract). |
| `GENERIC_BROLL` | Context/mood only. |

Realistic historical reconstruction MUST be routed through AI transparency review.
Reconstruction classified as `EVIDENCE_ASSET` is REJECTED/BLOCKED.
