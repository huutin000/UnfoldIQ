# UNFOLDIQ Channel Mascot Brief — CHAR_UNFOLDIQ_GUIDE_01

> Foundation contract (PRE POST-v1 01). No media generated. POST-v1 will validate with real Flow/Veo.

## Role

Recurring channel guide and brand anchor across UNFOLDIQ videos: intro/hook, chapter transitions,
difficult explanations, reaction/emphasis, outro. NOT an always-on-screen talking head; diagrams, maps,
evidence imagery, project characters, reconstructions, charts, and motion graphics remain the primary
content visuals. The mascot appears when guidance or brand recognition adds value.

## Channel mascot vs project character

- `CHAR_UNFOLDIQ_GUIDE_01` is GLOBAL_BRAND and never transforms into per-video characters
  (`CHAR_ANCIENT_MOTHER_01`, `CHAR_ROMAN_SOLDIER_01`, …). Project characters tell the story;
  the mascot guides the viewer through it.

## Direction

2.5D stylized adult human guide (27–33 apparent age): curious, calm, intelligent, approachable,
slightly playful, modern, never childish. Semi-cartoon clean shapes, subtle depth, soft volumetric
light, expressive eyes/brows, simplified realistic anatomy, readable silhouette. No photoreal human,
no uncanny skin, no chibi, no anime identity, no Pixar imitation, no stick figure, no micro-detail,
no brands/logos.

## Immutable identity

Face proportions, apparent age, hairstyle identity (short dark stable shape), body proportions
(simplified-realistic), art-style family (2.5D-stylized), core silhouette. See `channel-mascot.json`.

## Signature anchor

MUSTARD-YELLOW outerwear (light jacket/overshirt) over dark charcoal inner shirt; secondary accent
dark teal/deep green. Recognition at thumbnail size; stable Flow/Veo reference anchor.

## Wardrobe strategy (V1)

LOCK ONE core wardrobe first: `WARDROBE_GUIDE_CORE_01` (mustard outer, charcoal inner, dark-neutral
trousers/shoes, no logos/text). Default answer to "change clothes?" is NO. Future variants
(FIELD/LAB/FORMAL) require their own stable IDs plus approved/locked references; never invented
per video. None generated in this task.

## Content adaptation

Adapt via expression, gesture, pose, prop, environment, camera, diagram interaction, lighting —
never via new face, hairstyle, age, outfit, or art style. Science: points at spectrum/diagram.
History: stands beside timeline/map. Technology: interacts with UI/code diagram. Finance:
comparison chart/cards. Guide to content, not actor in every setting.

## Screen time

Hook optional/strong, intro likely, key explanations useful, transitions useful, evidence/
reconstruction usually absent, diagrams optional, outro likely. Content-driven; no fixed cadence rule.

## Style anchor

`STYLE_UNFOLDIQ_GUIDE_01`: 2.5D-stylized, clean-soft lines, moderate depth, low-medium detail,
soft-cinematic light, readable expression, simplified-realistic proportions. Drift into photoreal,
anime, chibi, clay, 3D-toy, or stick figure is forbidden without explicit rebrand.

## Expression set (semantic, identity-stable)

NEUTRAL, CURIOUS, THINKING, EXPLAINING, SURPRISED_LIGHT, CONCERNED, POSITIVE.

## Pose set (future reference targets, not generated yet)
